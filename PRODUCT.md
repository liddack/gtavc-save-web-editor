# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Players of classic GTA Vice City PC Retail who want to inspect and edit their own saves, including the owner's Hit the Courier save. Public open source distribution on GitHub Pages. Portuguese and English audiences.

## Product Purpose

Read, edit, review and download saves locally. Success means exact byte preservation on unchanged exports, bounded edits, clear compatibility diagnostics and transparent distinction between binary validation and in-game validation.

## Brand Personality

Precise, approachable, independent. Trust comes from visible before/after values and accurate validation status.

## Anti-references

No Rockstar branding, extracted game artwork, imitation game menus, decorative dashboards or promises of automatically completing campaigns.

## Design Principles

Keep the original untouched. Show the consequence of each change. Use familiar form controls. Make advanced data searchable and browsable. Distinguish statistics from actual progression.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable controls, explicit labels, visible focus, responsive layout, reduced-motion support and readable contrast. Both Portuguese and English are first-class.
